About This Tool

This website screenshot tool lets you capture any webpage as a high-quality PNG image. Simply enter a URL, choose your device type (desktop, mobile, or tablet), and click capture. The screenshot is generated instantly and you can download it directly.

How to Use

  1. Enter the full URL of the website you want to capture (include https://)
  2. Select your preferred device viewport (desktop, mobile, or tablet)
  3. Choose a delay time for pages that load slowly
  4. Click Capture Screenshot and wait a moment
  5. Preview the result and click Download PNG to save it

Use Cases

  • Web designers – Capture design references and portfolios
  • Marketers – Take screenshots for presentations and reports
  • Developers – Test responsive layouts across different viewports
  • Content creators – Capture website visuals for articles and reviews
  • Competitive research – Save snapshots of competitor websites

Features

  • ✅ Multiple viewport sizes (desktop, mobile, tablet)
  • ✅ Adjustable capture delay for dynamic content
  • ✅ High-quality PNG output
  • ✅ One-click download
  • ✅ No signup or registration required
