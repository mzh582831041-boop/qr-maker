How to Create a QR Code: Complete Guide for Beginners

June 2026 · 8 min read

QR codes have become essential for businesses, marketers, and individuals. From restaurant menus to business cards, QR codes provide a quick way to share information. This guide shows you exactly how to create and use QR codes effectively.

What Is a QR Code?

QR (Quick Response) code is a two-dimensional barcode that can be scanned by smartphones. Unlike traditional barcodes, QR codes store information both horizontally and vertically, allowing them to hold significantly more data—up to 4,296 alphanumeric characters.

QR codes were invented in 1994 by Denso Wave for tracking automotive parts. Today they are used worldwide for marketing, payments, authentication, and information sharing.

How QR Codes Work

When you scan a QR code with your smartphone camera, the device interprets the black and white pattern as data. The three large squares in the corners help the scanner orient the code, while the remaining pattern encodes the actual information. Modern smartphones have built-in QR scanning in their camera apps.

Best Practices

  • Ensure sufficient contrast between foreground and background
  • Maintain a clear quiet zone around the code
  • Test with multiple devices before mass printing
  • QR codes should be at least 2x2 cm for print

Common Use Cases

Business Cards

Add a QR code linking to your LinkedIn profile or portfolio. Recipients can instantly save your contact information.

Restaurant Menus

Contactless menus remain popular. QR codes link to digital menus that can be updated instantly.

WiFi Sharing

Create QR codes with WiFi credentials. Guests scan and connect without typing passwords.

Marketing Materials

Include QR codes in flyers and posters to drive traffic to landing pages or promotions.
